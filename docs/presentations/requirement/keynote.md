# Design for the worst day — Presentation Requirements

> Source: [`../keynote.html`](../keynote.html) · Type: Keynote · Audience: Product and design conference · Mode: Live · Time: 12 min
> Fictional case. The deck is the current implementation; this file captures the brief, structure, and acceptance.

## 1. Audience and intent

Product and design conference; mixed seniority.

**Intent:** Leave with one idea and three practices.

**Decision or action:** Try one practice in the next sprint.

**Success:** The audience can repeat 'the worst day is the product' and name one practice.

## 2. Narrative

Hook → one idea → evidence → pattern → counterpoint → practices → close.

### Slide sequence

| # | Slide | Budget | Purpose / speaker cue |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Design for the worst day | 1 | Open with the title and a pause. |
| 2 | The hook | 2 | Tell the story in first person if possible. |
| 3 | The idea | 2 | One sentence. |
| 4 | Evidence | 3 | Three short stories, one pattern. |
| 5 | The pattern | 2 | This is the argument. |
| 6 | The counterpoint | 2 | Acknowledge the obvious objection: this is not an argument against craft. |
| 7 | Three practices | 2 | Make the practices small enough to start. |
| 8 | Close | 1 | Pause. |

## 3. Evidence and fact-check

Three fictional stories, each with one concrete detail. Do not invent statistics; use the speaker's own evidence.

**Fact-check gate:** confirm names, numbers, dates, and comparisons before layout. A precise number without a date and source is a liability.

## 4. Language and delivery

Spoken, short sentences, repetition, pauses; no jargon; one idea.

## 5. Visual and accessibility

Dark warm, amber accent, large serif; minimal text; one image or metaphor per slide. Cover uses a stage photograph instead of a decorative SVG.

## 6. Time and structure

12 minutes; one to two minutes per slide. Must-cut: third example detail; protect the hook, the idea, and the close. (total budget: 15 minutes)

**Q&A:** prepare the ten most likely questions and one honest answer for each. Do not improvise a fact.

## 7. Verification

- **Titles-only read:** read the slide titles in order. The argument must hold without the body.
- **Timed run:** rehearse to a hard stop, record it, and cut from the must-cut list.
- **Evidence:** every number and comparison has a source, date, and range where relevant.
- **Visual:** check at presentation size, in grayscale, and in the exported file.
- **Accessibility:** alt text, reading order, contrast, color-not-alone, reduced motion, captions/transcript.
- **Print:** one slide per page, notes hidden, no clipped content.
- Read the titles; time the talk; record one run and cut 10%.

## 8. Non-goals and open questions

**Risks:** Too many ideas; a therapy tone; no action.

**Open:** Replace fictional stories with the speaker's own; confirm stage and AV.
