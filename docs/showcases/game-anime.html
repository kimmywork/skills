asteria — a bright world, dark panels where it matters

A landscape-first gacha game: the world screen carries the color and the scenery, menus switch to dark panels for focus, and every screen is designed and checked in English and Japanese. Kaisei Decol for titles, Zen Maru Gothic for controls, Zen Kaku Gothic New for text — the same three registers the page itself uses.

landscape 16:9 bright world · dark menus EN / 日本語 toggle display / UI / text faces rarity by frame + stars + label

Context

Who: players in portrait chat moments and landscape play sessions — the game is used mostly landscape, with menus that fill the wide frame. Jobs: explore and read quests, manage weapons, chase achievements, pull and share gacha results, inspect characters. Constraints: bright outdoor scenes with white text over them, dark menu panels for long sessions, and two languages that must both fit without truncation.

Principles

RuleRationale
The world is bright; the menus are darkScenery and gameplay stay colorful; long-reading and inventory surfaces switch to dark panels for focus and for the art to read against.
Rarity is frame + stars + labelColor is the first signal, never the only one; grayscale keeps the same information through borders and star counts.
Text over scenery needs a scrim or a shadowWhite dialogue over a bright field is measured against its worst-case backdrop, not a flat color.
EN and JP are both first-classThe same screen ships in both languages; buttons, quests, and item names are checked for fit and line height in each.
Reveals are anticipation, not a toll gateSummons can be replayed and skipped; nothing blocks the player from the result.

Motion and accessibility

Motion budget

  • Summon reveal staggers 60 ms per card and finishes under a second; the replay button re-runs it and nothing is gated.
  • Clouds drift slowly in the world screen; menus do not animate at all.
  • Reduce motion stops the drift, the reveal stagger, and any idle loops.

Accessibility

  • Rarity and alerts use frame, stars, and written labels, never hue alone; grayscale proof is built in.
  • White text over scenery is measured against the scrim's worst case, with a shadow only as a secondary aid.
  • Touch targets stay at least 44 px in this landscape layout; the action cluster sits in the lower-right thumb arc.
  • EN and JP both fit their containers; line heights and tracking are per-locale tokens.
